01 — About me

Research at the edge
of environment & humanity

I am a 4th year PhD candidate in Regional Science & Economic Geography at the Gran Sasso Science Institute in L'Aquila, Italy. My work sits at the critical intersection of environment, disaster, and health in Africa — combining quantitative rigour with qualitative depth.

Extreme temperatures and mental health. Flood trauma and community resilience. The intergenerational financial burden known as Black tax. These are the questions I pursue — because they are the questions that matter most to the communities I study.

My background spans development corporations, agri-food systems, and migration research — all animated by the same vision: building evidence where it is most urgently needed.
