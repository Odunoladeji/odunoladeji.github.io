About Me

I am a 4th year PhD candidate in Regional Science & Economic Geography at Gran Sasso Science Institute, L'Aquila, Italy. I am all for social impact—researching the things that keep communities up, either in the day or at night. Like how extreme temperatures meddle with our well-being, or the trauma that comes with experiencing floods. Combining numbers with narratives, my work is dedicated to building evidence at the critical intersection of environment, disaster, and health in Africa. When I’m not mapping those complex systems, I’m exploring the psychological dimensions of Black tax—a critical inroad to understanding intergenerational dynamics in the Black community. In case you have seen my contribution to development corporations, agri-food systems, and migration, it is all the same cause, driven by the same vision.
